Security Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Report a Vulnerability

Please do not post vulnerabilities publicly. Report privately to your security contact email.

Expected Process

  • Acknowledgment target: within 48 hours.
  • Severity assessment and patch planning.
  • Coordinated disclosure after fix.

Deployment Best Practices

  • Use HTTPS for all traffic.
  • Enable rate limiting and strict CORS on CAD backend.
  • Keep dependencies and DWG converter binaries up to date.
  • Never commit secrets to source control.
